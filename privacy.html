Privacy Policy

CH Content Analytics is a private analytics tool used by the account owner to export TikTok performance metrics.

Data we access

  • Basic TikTok account information (e.g., user identifier / username)
  • Video list
  • Video performance metrics (e.g., views, likes, comments, shares and related fields)

How we use data

We use this data only to generate analytics and reporting for the connected TikTok account.

Sharing

We do not sell or share TikTok data with third parties.

Storage & retention

Access tokens may be stored securely to retrieve metrics. Reports/metrics may be stored for reporting purposes.

Revoking access / deletion

You can revoke access via your TikTok settings at any time. You can also request deletion of stored tokens/report data by emailing cj21062018@gmail.com.
